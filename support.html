Pip’s Playhouse Support

Help for parents and teachers

Pip’s Playhouse is a learning app for young children, hosted by Pip the parrot. It has stories, science experiments, letter tracing, counting, puzzles, thinking games and more.

Common questions

Does it need the internet? No. The iPhone, iPad and Android apps include everything and work offline.

Are there ads or purchases? No ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

Can more than one child play? Yes. Add a player for each child when the app opens. Each player has their own stars and progress.

Pip isn’t talking. Check that the Voice switch at the top of the game shelf is on and the device volume is up.

How do I remove a player? On the player list that appears when the app opens, delete that player. Their stars, progress, paintings and puzzle photos are removed from the device.

Privacy

Nothing a child types or makes leaves the device. Read the full privacy policy.
