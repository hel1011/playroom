Pip’s Playhouse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Pip’s Playhouse is a learning app for young children. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it is kept. It covers the iPhone and iPad app, the Android app and the web version.

The short version

Everything a child enters or creates in Pip’s Playhouse stays on the device it was entered on. The app does not send it to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no in-app purchases.

What the app keeps on the device

  • Player name and age. When a player is added, the app asks for a name and an age so it can show each child their own stars and progress. Any name can be used, such as a first name or a nickname.
  • Progress. Stars earned, games and stories finished, treehouse items, and settings such as voice and sound on or off.
  • Pictures made in the app. Paintings made in the Paint game.
  • Photos chosen for Photo Puzzle. If someone adds a photo to make a puzzle, the app keeps a smaller copy of that photo on the device so it can be played again.
  • Technical notes. A few small notes, such as which game was open, so the app can pick up where a child left off if it closes unexpectedly.

This information is stored only in the app’s storage on the device. We cannot see it and we do not collect it.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera or photo library only when someone taps “Add photos” in Photo Puzzle. The chosen photo is used to make the puzzle and stays on the device. The app does not upload photos anywhere.

Internet use

The iPhone, iPad and Android apps include all of their games, stories, pictures and voices, and they work without an internet connection. They do not contain links to websites, advertising, or third-party services.

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. When the web version is loaded, GitHub may receive technical information such as the visitor’s IP address, as described in GitHub’s own privacy statement. We do not receive or use that information.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose information about children or anyone else, because the app does not send us any.

Deleting information

  • To remove one player along with their stars, progress, paintings and puzzle photos, delete that player from the player list that appears when the app opens.
  • To remove a puzzle photo, tap the × on the photo in Photo Puzzle.
  • To remove everything, delete the app from the device (for the web version, clear the website’s data in the browser settings).

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
