## Description (both stores)

Pip's Playhouse is a bright, friendly learning app for little kids, hosted by Pip the parrot. Pip talks children through every game in a warm, natural voice, so kids who can't read yet can still play on their own.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Story Time: 11 read-along picture books. Words light up as Pip reads, and the answer choices light up as Pip reads each question.
• Science Lab: 34 hands-on experiments, from volcanoes, magnets and rainbows to firing a cannon, making a tornado, the phases of the moon, pond life under a microscope, fossils, bees, the solar system and how vaccines help your body.
• Town Helpers: join sisters Ellie and Zoey and their dog Cooper as they help eight neighbors around town, counting, matching numbers, sounding out letters, planting, shopping, recycling, crossing the street safely and caring for a pet. Progress is saved along the way.
• At the Doctor: go through Zoey's checkup step by step: weighing, measuring, an eye test, a hearing test, an ear check and a shot, then a sticker and a lollipop. A gentle way to get ready for a real visit.
• What Should You Do?: 36 everyday situations, like sharing, staying safe, healthy snacks and big feelings. Pip reads each answer aloud and lights it up, so kids who can't read yet can choose on their own.
• Hide and Seek: Pip hides somewhere in a living room or a bedroom. Kids follow his clues, which get more helpful with every wrong guess, until they find him.
• Piano Songs: play seven favorite songs on a color-coded piano by following the glowing keys, including Chopsticks with two keys at once, or make up your own music.
• Guitar: tap a color on the guitar's neck to pick a chord, then swipe across the strings to strum. Play along with 12 favorite songs, from Twinkle, Twinkle to Jingle Bells, as colored notes slide in. Easy uses two or three chords, Hard uses five. Or strum your own music.
• Trace It: trace capital letters, lowercase letters and numbers.
• Count With Me, Add & Take Away and Dot-to-Dot for early math.
• Rhyme Time for early reading.
• Think It Through: detective clues, sorting and "make it true" logic puzzles.
• Memory Match games with animals, opposites and colors.
• Photo Puzzle: jigsaw puzzles from 4 to 12 pieces, including puzzles made from your own photos.
• Paint, Balloon Pop and Tic-Tac-Toe.
• Pip's Treehouse: earn stars by playing, then spend them decorating Pip's treehouse, inside and out. Save up for super special things like a puppy that follows Pip around, a hot air balloon ride, fireworks, a weather machine and a room that turns into outer space. Inside, save up for six arcade cabinets you can really play: Moon Landing, Turbo Race, Free Kick, Brick Breaker, Skee-Ball and Table Tennis. Kids can also drag Pip anywhere in his treehouse.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• A separate player for each child, with their own stars and progress.
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts.
• Works without an internet connection.
• Nothing a child types or makes leaves the device.
